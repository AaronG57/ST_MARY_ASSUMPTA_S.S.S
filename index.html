About the school

A girls' secondary school in Pakele

St. Mary Assumpta S.S.S. is officially listed in Uganda government education records as a government secondary school in Adjumani District, with EMIS code 12058.

The Ministry of Education's school-placement information identifies St. Mary Assumpta S.S. with selection code 1006, at O-Level, in the girls/boarding category.

The school's current public identity is St. Mary Assumpta Girls Senior Secondary School. The school's own website gives its postal address as P.O. Box 12, Adjumani, Uganda.

The school is associated with Pakele in Adjumani District. The Diocese of Arua's education directory also lists St. Mary Assumpta Girls' Secondary School under Pakele Parish.

Verification note: Historical Ministry headcount reports contain older student totals, but those figures are not presented here as current enrollment because a recent official enrollment figure could not be independently verified.
